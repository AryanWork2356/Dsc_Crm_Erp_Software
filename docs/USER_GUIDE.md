# DSC Interior ERP – User guide

*For the people of DSC Interior. No technical knowledge needed.*

## 1. What is this?

Until now DSC's work lived in many places – WhatsApp chats, Excel sheets, Google Drive, a notebook, the accountant's software. This system is **one place for everything**. Each person signs in and sees only what their job needs. Because it is all one system, you **never type the same thing twice**: a quotation becomes a project, a BOQ becomes a purchase request, a delivery adds to stock, stock sent to site becomes project cost, and project cost becomes profit – automatically.

It works in a web browser (laptop, tablet or phone). Nothing to install: open the address your admin gives you and sign in.

## 2. Getting around (everyone)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Menu (left side)** | Shows only the sections you may use. On a phone, tap ☰ at the top left. |
| **Search box (top)** | Type a name, phone number or ID (like *PRJ-00001*) to find a client, lead, project, vendor, PO, invoice, document… |
| **🔔 Bell** | Your alerts: approvals waiting, follow-ups due, material received, payment due… Click one to jump to it. |
| **Approvals** | Anything waiting for *your* decision. Approve or reject (a reason is needed to reject). |
| **Status tags** | Coloured tags (Draft, Pending approval, Approved, Paid, Overdue…) mean the same thing on every screen. |
| **Red words** | Overdue, delayed, low stock, over budget – these need attention. |

**Good habits:** fill in the information when you do the work (not at the end of the week); use *Log call / note* on leads so the whole team knows what was said; never share your password – every action is recorded under your name.

## 3. One job from start to finish

*Example: Mrs. Deshpande wants a 3BHK interior at Lodha Splendora, Thane.*

| Step | Who | What they do | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sales** | Adds her as a **Lead** (source: Instagram, ₹28 lakh, 3BHK). Sets a follow-up for tomorrow. | CRM → Leads |
| 2 | Sales | Calls, logs the call. Schedules a **site visit**. After the visit, records measurements. | CRM → Site visits |
| 3 | **Sales / Designer** | Builds the **Quotation** line by line (kitchen, wardrobes, ceiling, electrical, paint, flooring…). GST adds up by itself. Clicks **Submit for approval**. | Sales → Quotations |
| 4 | **Director** | Sees it in **Approvals**, checks, approves. | Approvals |
| 5 | Sales | Clicks **Mark as sent**, downloads the PDF, shares it (WhatsApp/email). Lead moves to *Quotation sent*. | Quotation page |
| 6 | Sales | Client agrees → **Client accepted**. Lead becomes **Won**. | Quotation page |
| 7 | **Director / PM** | **Convert to project**: picks the project manager and dates. The system creates the project and a draft **BOQ**. | Quotation page |
| 8 | **Project Manager** | Opens the BOQ, enters the cost of material and labour per item, submits it. Sets the **budget from BOQ**. Adds tasks (demolition, wiring, ceiling, kitchen…) and milestones (30% advance, 30% carcass, 30% finishing, 10% handover). | Projects, Sales → BOQ |
| 9 | **Accounts** | At the first milestone, clicks **Invoice from milestone**. Director approves; the invoice is issued. | Project → Milestones, Finance |
| 10 | Accounts | Client pays → **Record payment**. Balance updates; status becomes *Paid*. | Finance → Invoices |
| 11 | **Site engineer** | Creates a **Purchase request** from the BOQ ("18mm plywood × 60 sheets"). PM approves (small) or Management (larger). | Procurement → Requests |
| 12 | **Procurement** | Turns it into a **Purchase order** for Greenply. Approval by amount. Sends the PDF to the vendor. | Procurement → Orders |
| 13 | **Store keeper** | Truck arrives: **Receive material** against the PO – counts received, damaged, rejected. Only good stock is added. | Inventory → Receipts |
| 14 | Store keeper | **Issue to site** – stock moves to the project's site store and the cost is charged to the project. | Inventory → Issues |
| 15 | **Supervisor** | Every morning marks **attendance** for the site. Wages are calculated automatically. Workers mark themselves from their phones. | Workforce → Attendance |
| 16 | Workers | Open their phone screen: *Mark attendance*, *My tasks* (Start / Done), *Need material*, *Report problem*. | Worker app |
| 17 | **PM** | Updates tasks; project progress rises. Opens **Profit & budget** any time to see cost vs budget and margin. | Project → Profit & budget |
| 18 | Client | Logs into the **client portal**: sees progress, photos, approves a finished stage, checks invoices. | Client portal |
| 19 | PM | Handover → marks the project **Completed**. The 12-month **warranty** starts. | Project |
| 20 | **Support** | A client reports a loose hinge → ticket is opened (warranty detected automatically), assigned, resolved. | Customer support |

At any moment the **Owner** sees on the Dashboard: projects, delays, sales, receivables, payables, stock value, profit.

## 4. Your day, by role

### Owner / Director
- **Dashboard** each morning: red alerts at the top ("3 quotations awaiting approval", "2 projects delayed", "₹4.8 L overdue", "5 materials low", "PRJ-002 is 12% over budget") – click to go straight to it. Change the period (Today / Week / Month / Quarter / Year / Custom).
- **Approvals** – clear the queue. Large purchases and payments come to the Owner.
- **Reports** – profitability, budget variance, receivables, sales; export to Excel/PDF.
- **Settings** – approval limits, company details, users, payroll rules, WhatsApp, Audit log (who changed what).

### Sales
- **Leads:** new enquiries → log calls → schedule follow-ups → site visit → quotation. Use the **Pipeline** board and drag cards between stages. **Follow-ups** page = who to call today.
- **Quotations:** build, submit, send, track (Viewed / Negotiation / Accepted). **Revise** instead of editing after approval.
- **WhatsApp** inbox: reply to customers, use templates, leave internal notes.
- You see only **your own** leads.

### Designer
- Site visits and measurements; help prepare quotations and BOQs (quantities and selling rates; cost columns stay hidden); upload drawings to the project's **Documents**; your assigned tasks.

### Project Manager
- **Dashboard:** my projects, my tasks, workers on site today, approvals waiting (requests on *your* projects).
- Per project: **tasks** (with "depends on"), **milestones** (with billing %), **BOQ**, **Materials** (plan vs bought vs issued vs used), **Profit & budget**, documents.
- Watch for the red **Delayed** and **Over budget** tags.

### Site engineer / Supervisor
- Mark **attendance** (Present / Half / Absent / Leave + overtime) – before 11 am ideally.
- Raise **purchase requests**; log **expenses**; update tasks; record **material consumed**; upload site photos.

### Procurement
- **Purchase requests** that are approved → make **purchase orders**. Keep vendor details and ratings updated.
- **Deliveries** page: what is expected, what is late (call the vendor!). **Low stock** alerts tell you what to reorder.

### Store keeper
- **Receive material** against POs (count carefully – damaged and rejected are recorded). **Issue to site** and **Return from site**. **Stock** page shows warehouse and site stock; corrections need a reason.

### Accounts
- **Invoices** (including from milestones), **Payments received**, **Expenses** (approve/record), **Vendor bills** and payments, **Receivables / Payables** by ageing, **Finance overview** for cash in/out. Export to Excel for your CA.

### HR
- **Employees** and their documents, **Leave** requests, **Payroll** (set rules once → *Run payroll* monthly → payslips as PDF → adjust bonus/incentive), **Announcements**, **Workers** and **Contractors**, **Attendance**.

### Admin
- Users and roles, company settings, **Import data** (clients, leads, vendors, materials, workers, employees from Excel), documents, audit log, automation.

### Workers (phone)
1. Sign in with the ID and password HR gave you.
2. Tap the green **MARK ATTENDANCE** button when you reach site.
3. Under **My tasks** tap **Start**, then **Done** (or **Stuck** if you cannot continue).
4. **Need material** – type what and how many. **Report problem** – tell your supervisor instantly.

### Clients and vendors
- **Clients** get their own link: project progress, photos, stage approvals, quotations (Accept / Request changes), invoices, payment history, support requests.
- **Vendors** see only their purchase orders, update delivery dates, check payments, and message DSC.

## 5. Routines

**Every day:** Sales – follow-ups; Supervisors – attendance; Store – receive/issue as it happens; PM – open the dashboard; Everyone – clear *Approvals* and the 🔔.
**Every week:** PM reviews delayed tasks and materials; Procurement chases late deliveries; Accounts reviews receivables; Owner reviews profitability.
**Every month:** HR runs payroll; Accounts exports invoices/payments/expenses for the CA; Owner reviews reports and the audit log.

## 6. Important rules (why the software sometimes says "no")

- **Approved documents are locked.** To change an approved quotation, BOQ or PO use *Revise* / create a new one – so the numbers the client agreed to can't silently change.
- **You can't approve your own request.** The Owner can.
- **Stock can't go below zero.** If it says "not enough", check the receipt or the location.
- **Attendance can't be marked for the future**, and a worker can't be on two sites in one day.
- **Corrections need a reason** (stock adjustments, damaged goods, rejections) – it goes in the audit log.
- **Money only counts when approved** – an expense or invoice doesn't affect project profit until approved/issued.
- **You only see what your job needs.** If a menu is missing, ask the Owner or Admin.

## 7. Setting up for real use (first-day checklist for the Admin)

1. **Settings → Company:** legal name, address, GSTIN, PAN, bank details, number prefixes, default GST %, **approval limits**.
2. **Settings → Team & Users:** create real users with the right roles; deactivate demo users. Reset the Owner password.
3. **Import data:** bring in clients, leads, vendors, materials, workers and employees from Excel (download the template for each).
4. **Inventory → Locations:** create your warehouses. **Materials:** check the catalogue, set reorder levels.
5. **HR:** set each employee's salary structure; **Payroll → Payroll rules** (working days, overtime rate, PF %).
6. **Terms & conditions** templates for quotations and POs (Admin can edit the text in the database/seed or ask your developer to add a screen).
7. **WhatsApp** (optional): Settings → WhatsApp Connection with your Meta Business details.
8. Ask your developer to schedule the **daily automation** and the database/file **backups**.

## 8. Common questions

**I made a mistake on an approved quotation.** Open it → *Revise*. The old version is kept.
**A number looks wrong.** Every screen reads from the same records; open the item and check its history, or ask the Admin to look in the **Audit log**.
**I can't see a project/lead.** You only see ones assigned to you; ask your manager to add you.
**Can I use it on my phone?** Yes. Workers get a special simple screen; everyone else gets the normal screens in a phone-friendly layout.
**Where are my files?** *Documents* in the menu, or the *Documents* tab inside a project / client / vendor.
**The client wants a copy.** Quotation, invoice and PO each have a **PDF** button.
