# Site supervisor – quick guide / Supervisor ke liye aasan guide

Open the DSC app on your phone. You land on **Daily site reports**.
*Phone par DSC app kholiye. Pehle screen par "Daily site reports" dikhega.*

## Every day, 2 reports
| When | Button | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **By 10:00 AM** (outstation sites 9:30) | **Send morning update** | 1. Press **+** for how many people are on site. 2. Write today's plan in one line. 3. Choose material: green / amber / red. 4. **Send**. |
| **Before leaving site** (by 7 PM) | **Send evening report** | 1. People on site. 2. What work was done. 3. **Take or choose photos** (at least one). 4. Move the slider to how much of the site is finished. 5. Material: green / amber / red. 6. **Send**. |

*Subah 10 baje tak morning update, shaam ko jaane se pehle evening report (photo ke saath).*

## Material colours
- **Green** – all material is here.
- **Amber** – material will run short in a day or two.
- **Red** – work has stopped, no material. *(The project manager and procurement are told at once.)*

## Other things you do in the app
- **Need material?** Menu → Procurement → Purchase Requests → New. Ask **at least 2 days before** you need it.
- **Material arrived?** Inventory → Material Receipts → record it and **take a photo of the challan**.
- **Moving workers or material to another site?** Menu → Projects → Transfers → New transfer. The project manager must approve.
- **Client asks for extra work?** Menu → Projects → Extra Work → New. **Do not start** until it says *Client approved*.
- **Problem?** Write it in the "Any problem to report?" box. Your project manager gets it immediately.

## If something goes wrong
- *No internet*: the app shows "No internet connection" or an error when you press Send. Keep the page open, move to a place with signal and press **Send** again. (Saving reports on the phone while offline is planned for a later version.)
- Sent by mistake? Open the same report again and press **Send** – it updates, it does not duplicate.
- Late report? It still goes through, marked "late". Always better late than missing.

## Why this matters
Your reports show on the directors' screen and count towards your project's performance score: reports sent, sent on time, material problems raised early.
*Aapki report directors ko dikhti hai aur project ke performance score mein judti hai.*
